Mohammad Movahedi

Construction & Infrastructure Management Researcher

I am a PhD candidate working on applications of AI in human-AI interaction, human behavior modeling, transportation planning, infrastructure & construction managment, and asset management at the FAMU-FSU College of Engineering, affiliated with the RIDER Center.

My research focuses on data-driven methods for understanding, managing, and improving transportation and infrastructure systems under uncertainty. Explore my published work in Research, my academic CV and projects in Background, technical tutorials in Scratchpad, and hosted academic seminars in Webinars.

Research Interests

  • Transportation & Infrastructure Planning: Data-driven decision support, traffic flow optimization, and adaptive traffic control.
  • Resiliencey: Agent-based modeling, hurricane evacuation dynamics, and critical infrastructure lifelines.
  • Digital Twins: Extended reality and generative AI for building component inspection and automated asset management.
  • Applied AI & ML: Agentic architectures, tool use, and local language model deployment.
