Human Behavior Modeling

This scratchpad collects a planned series on human behavior modeling for transportation, urban planning, safety, sustainability, and policy research.

The series is designed as a study flow: start with simple descriptive models, move into psychological and social mechanisms, then connect those mechanisms to choice models, intervention design, and system-level simulation.
