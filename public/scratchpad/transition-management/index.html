Transition Management

Welcome to the Transition Management curriculum. This 12-part series is an advanced, academic exploration of how massive socio-technical shifts occur, how disruptive innovations reshape industries, and the strategic frameworks leaders use to navigate these turbulent periods.

If you are a student, researcher, or strategist attempting to understand why powerful incumbents fail or how niche technologies overtake entire markets, this series provides a comprehensive, interconnected playbook. We bridge the gap between sociological theory (like the Multi-Level Perspective and Diffusion of Innovations) and actionable business strategy (like Blue Ocean and Jobs-to-be-Done).

Course Philosophy

Transitions are not purely technological phenomena; they are socio-technical. A superior technology does not automatically win. A transition only succeeds when technology, user psychology, regulatory policy, and ecosystem economics align.
