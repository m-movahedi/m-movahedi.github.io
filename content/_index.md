I am a PhD candidate working on transportation planning, infrastructure systems, traffic engineering, urban mobility, and asset management at the **FAMU-FSU College of Engineering**, affiliated with the **RIDER Center**.

My research focuses on data-driven methods for understanding, managing, and improving transportation and infrastructure systems under uncertainty. Explore my published work in [Research](/research/), my academic CV and projects in [Background](/background/), technical tutorials in [Scratchpad](/scratchpad/), hosted academic seminars in [Webinars](/webinars/), and long-form writing in the [Blog](/blog/).

## Research Interests

- **[Transportation Planning & ITS](/research/)**: Data-driven decision support, traffic flow optimization, and adaptive traffic control.
- **[Disaster Resilience & Evacuation](/research/)**: Agent-based modeling, hurricane evacuation dynamics, and critical infrastructure lifelines.
- **[Digital Twins & Inspection XR](/research/generative-ai-virtual-reality-a-training-tool)**: Extended reality and generative AI for building component inspection and automated asset management.
- **[Applied AI & Local LLM Workflows](/scratchpad/local-llms/)**: Agentic architectures, tool use, and local language model deployment.
